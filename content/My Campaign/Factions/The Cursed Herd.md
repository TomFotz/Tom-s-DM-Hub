[Thrymrir] that created The Cursed Herd - a multispecies hoard of gnolls, mintaurs, deer, cattle and maybe a few of your mouse kin and other herd like creatures that have been raiding and terrorizing the region.

The leader of The Cursed Herd is [[Tirnus Kobral]] a Death Knight. His calling is to fulfill the prophecy of the [[Purge]] and create an [[Army of the Reclaimed]]

A handful of [[Oggie May Goodbeam]] distant family has fallen under this curse , and they need a rescue.
